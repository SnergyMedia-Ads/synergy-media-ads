FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining the Synergy partner network.

What is the minimum monthly Meta ad spend required?

Our growth retainers work best for brands investing at least $2,000/month in Meta ad spend. For brands launching from scratch, our Foundation ($1,500 One-Time) tier sets up tracking and initial testing structures properly.

Do you create the video and static ad creatives?

Yes. On our Growth and Scale retainers, we provide creative briefs, scriptwriting, hook testing formulas, and editing guidance. You provide raw product clips or UGC, and our team engineers them into high-converting Meta ads.

Are there long-term contracts?

No. All our monthly retainers operate on rolling 30-day agreements. We believe in earning your business month after month through measurable ROAS and transparent weekly communication.

How does your ad account unban & protection work?

We audit your Business Manager, verify business identities, align campaigns strictly with Meta’s Advertising Policies, and submit direct compliance escalations if restrictions occur.
